📖 About

This project uses a Machine Learning model trained on cow and buffalo images to predict the breed from a photo. Powered by TensorFlow.js and Teachable Machine. Cattle are bovine animals used for meat, milk, and labor, while buffaloes are larger and mainly used for meat and milk. Despite their differences, both are essential for food and livelihood worldwide.
